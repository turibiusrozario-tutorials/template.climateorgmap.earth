Start a Climate Organization Map for your region

This map is free to copy: your own map, Google Sheet, forms and point people, in about 15 minutes, all in the browser.

  1. Fork the template on GitHub: template.climateorgmap.earth. Turn on Actions, and set Settings → Pages → Source to GitHub Actions.
  2. Edit one file, map.config.ts: your map's name, your state's two-letter code and your land acknowledgement.
  3. Open your site's setup.html. It walks you through the rest and checks each step.
  4. Make a Google Sheet, paste the sheet script, and run Set up sheet + forms. It builds the forms.
  5. Publish the sheet as a web app and paste its URL into map.config.ts.

The full steps, getting updates, a climateorgmap.earth address and handover are in the template's README.
